01 — The standard

The bar you are applying to hold.

Six parts of the call. One profession. You learn the offer, you practice to a visible bar, and you leave knowing where the skill is meant to point. That is the work. The application asks if you will actually do it.

  1. 01

    Master the offer

    Know the promise, the proof, and the person in front of you. The closer who improvises the product sounds expensive and cheap at the same time. You carry the offer. You stop guessing.

  2. 02

    Close with certainty

    Open with authority. Diagnose cleanly. Handle “I need to think about it” without begging. Certainty is trained. It is not a mood you wait for on the call.

  3. 03

    Work from anywhere

    The profession is the conversation. A quiet room, a calendar, and a standard you can carry. Remote high-ticket closing does not depend on the building you happen to sit in.

  4. 04

    Practice to a standard

    Reps are reviewed. The bar is visible. You meet it, or you hear why you did not. Theory does not survive contact with a recorded call.

  5. 05

    Run the frame

    Pace, questions, and silence. You lead without chasing. The person on the other end should feel guided toward a decision, not cornered into one.

  6. 06

    Placement direction

    When the standard is in your voice, we point you toward offers that match how you close. Direction. Not a guaranteed seat, and not a fantasy pipeline.
